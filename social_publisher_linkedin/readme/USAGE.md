To use this module, you need to:

- Write, approve and schedule posts as described in *Social Publisher*; posts
  of a LinkedIn account are published to that profile or page.
- Hashtags written as `#Tag` stay clickable on LinkedIn.
- On a company page account, click **Import Posts** to bring the posts that
  are already on the page into Odoo, with their metrics.
- **Sync Metrics** on the account, or **Refresh Metrics** on a post, reads the
  latest numbers; a daily job does it for recent posts.
