- The past posts of a personal profile cannot be imported: LinkedIn restricts
  the permission to list them. Company pages can be imported.
- Comments and reactions are not shown or answered from Odoo.
- On LinkedIn's *Development tier* the Community Management API allows 100 calls
  per member a day; a profile post costs 10 calls per metrics refresh, so keep
  the account's metrics window short until the app reaches the *Standard tier*.
