LinkedIn provider for *Social Publisher*. It connects personal profiles
and company pages through LinkedIn's official API, publishes approved
posts with an optional image and brings the analytics of every post back
into Odoo.

For company pages it can also import the posts that are already on the
page.

Configuration
=============

To configure this module, you need to:

- Create your app(s) at https://www.linkedin.com/developers/apps:

  - **Personal profile:** an app with the *Share on LinkedIn* and *Sign
    In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect* products (self-service). Post
    analytics need the *Community Management API*, which LinkedIn
    reviews and only grants to an app with no other product, so use a
    second app for it. If that app also has ``w_member_social`` and
    ``r_basicprofile``, it can run the profile alone.
  - **Company page:** the *Community Management API* app only. The
    person who connects must be an administrator of the page.

- Go to *Social > Configuration > Accounts*, create an account with the
  *LinkedIn* provider and choose *Personal Profile* or *Company Page*.
- On the *LinkedIn* tab, fill in the Client ID and Client Secret of your
  app(s), and add the *Redirect URL* shown there to the *Auth* tab of
  every LinkedIn app you use.
- Click **Connect** (and **Connect Metrics** for a profile that uses two
  apps). For a company page, pick the page among those you administer.

Access lasts about 60 days. The responsible user gets a to-do a week
before it expires; reconnect with one click.

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

- Write, approve and schedule posts as described in *Social Publisher*;
  posts of a LinkedIn account are published to that profile or page.
- Hashtags written as ``#Tag`` stay clickable on LinkedIn.
- On a company page account, click **Import Posts** to bring the posts
  that are already on the page into Odoo, with their metrics.
- **Sync Metrics** on the account, or **Refresh Metrics** on a post,
  reads the latest numbers; a daily job does it for recent posts.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The past posts of a personal profile cannot be imported: LinkedIn
  restricts the permission to list them. Company pages can be imported.
- Comments and reactions are not shown or answered from Odoo.
- On LinkedIn's *Development tier* the Community Management API allows
  100 calls per member a day; a profile post costs 10 calls per metrics
  refresh, so keep the account's metrics window short until the app
  reaches the *Standard tier*.
