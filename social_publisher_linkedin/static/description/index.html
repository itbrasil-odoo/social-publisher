Social Publisher - LinkedIn

Publish to LinkedIn profiles and company pages, with analytics

Adds LinkedIn to Social Publisher: connect personal profiles and company pages from Odoo, publish approved posts with an image at the scheduled time, and bring the analytics of every post back into Odoo.

Connect from the account form

  • Personal profile: publishing, plus post analytics when your app has the Community Management API.
  • Company page: after connecting, pick one of the pages you administer. Publish as the page, import its existing posts and read its analytics.
  • Access lasts about 60 days; the responsible user gets a reminder before it expires and reconnects in one click.

Analytics in Odoo

Impressions, reach, reactions, comments, reshares and clicks per post, refreshed daily, plus account totals over the last 28 days.

Requirements

  • Your own app at linkedin.com/developers; the account form shows the redirect URL to register.
  • Profiles: the Share on LinkedIn and Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect products (self-service).
  • Company pages and analytics: the Community Management API product, which LinkedIn reviews and only grants to an app with no other product.
  • When that app also has w_member_social and r_basicprofile, personal profiles can use it alone and connect once for publishing and analytics.
  • The past posts of a personal profile cannot be imported: LinkedIn restricts that permission. Company pages can.

Data goes only between your Odoo and the LinkedIn API, with the access you grant. Not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.

Questions and bug reports: GitHub issues.
