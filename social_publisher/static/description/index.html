Social Publisher

Plan, approve, schedule and measure your social media posts

A content calendar for the posts of your company pages and of your team, with an approval step before anything goes public and the metrics of every post in one place. Networks plug in as separate modules; install Social Publisher LinkedIn to publish on LinkedIn.

A calendar of everything that goes out

Drafts, approved and published posts on one calendar, coloured by status. Drag a post to another day to reschedule it.

An approval step you can trust

  • Writers create and edit drafts but cannot approve or publish. Give this group to an assistant or an AI agent that drafts posts for you.
  • Managers approve and publish. Editing the text, image or account of an approved post sends it back to draft.
  • Approved posts are published automatically at the scheduled time. A failed publication is flagged and never retried on its own.
  • An optional required opening blocks publication if the first paragraph was changed.

Metrics for every post

Impressions, reach, reactions, comments and more are collected daily, with a history per post and per account. Use the list, pivot and graph views to compare topics and weeks.

Also included

  • Multi-company: each account belongs to a company, or is shared by all of them.
  • Tags, languages, internal notes and chatter on every post.
  • A to-do for the responsible user a week before an account's access expires.
  • Built on Odoo Community: no Enterprise module required. English and Brazilian Portuguese.

This module stores everything in your database and sends no data anywhere by itself; network modules only talk to the network you connect.
